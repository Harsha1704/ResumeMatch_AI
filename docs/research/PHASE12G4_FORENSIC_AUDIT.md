## 5. Statistical Uncertainty & Independence Audit (Task 4)

1. **Pooled McNemar Test ($N=1,480$):**
   - Repeating 296 validation pairs across 5 training seeds evaluates model initialization stability, **not** sample generalization across 1,480 independent recruitment pairs.
   - *Certified Classification:* **Exploratory descriptive evidence**. It does not establish independent statistical confirmation.
2. **Multiple-Testing Family-Wise Correction:**
   - Seed 42: Unadjusted $p = 0.0152 \implies \text{Holm } p = \mathbf{0.0745}$
   - Seed 43: Unadjusted $p = 0.0230 \implies \text{Holm } p = \mathbf{0.0745}$
   - Seed 44: Unadjusted $p = 0.8323 \implies \text{Holm } p = \mathbf{0.8323}$ (Optimization minimum)
   - Seed 45: Unadjusted $p = 0.0250 \implies \text{Holm } p = \mathbf{0.0745}$
   - Seed 46: Unadjusted $p = 0.0149 \implies \text{Holm } p = \mathbf{0.0745}$
   - *Finding:* Four unadjusted $p < 0.05$ tests do not constitute four independent replications. Under family-wise error rate control, the adjusted $p$-values are marginally significant ($p_{\text{adj}} \approx 0.0745$).
3. **Dependence-Aware Bootstrap Uncertainty (Seed 42, $B=10,000$ Resamples):**
   - **Candidate-Clustered (91 clusters):** 95% CI = `[+2.47%, +13.85%]`, $P(\Delta > 0) = 99.78\%$
   - **Job-Clustered (52 clusters):** 95% CI = `[+1.12%, +13.99%]`, $P(\Delta > 0) = 99.01\%$
   - **Two-Way Clustered (Candidate + Job simultaneous):** 95% CI = `[0.00%, +15.38%]`, $P(\Delta > 0) = 97.45\%$
   - *Finding:* Candidate and job dependence widen the uncertainty intervals, but the accuracy gain remains strictly positive ($>99\%$ probability of superiority).
