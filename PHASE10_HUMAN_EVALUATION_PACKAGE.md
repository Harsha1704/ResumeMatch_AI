## 1. Evaluation Protocol & Blinding Methodology

To obtain authentic, unbiased human assessment of ResumeMatch AI's user-facing outputs, an independent double-blind evaluation protocol has been designed.

### 1.1 Blinding Procedure
1. **Anonymized Identifiers:** All candidate personal names, real email addresses, and university identities are assigned anonymized study codes (`AUDIT-CAND-01` through `AUDIT-CAND-30`).
2. **Module Blindness:** Evaluators are presented with paired input (Resume + Job Description) and system output (Career Mentor Dossier + Interview Studio Questions and Answers) without indicating whether answers were generated by deterministic NLP rules, curated expert banks, or human coaches.
3. **Independent Rater Panel:** Evaluators should consist of three independent groups:
   - **Group A (Senior Technical Hiring Managers):** Evaluates technical accuracy, architectural depth, and interview readiness.
   - **Group B (Professional Career Coaches):** Evaluates counseling tone, actionability, prerequisite sequencing, and roadmap feasibility.
   - **Group C (Engineering Students & Job Seekers):** Evaluates clarity, educational usefulness, and confidence building.

## 2. Standardized 5-Dimension Scoring Rubric

Evaluators grade each scenario on a standardized scale from **1 to 10** across five objective dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Evidence Grounding & Truthfulness (Weight: 25%)
- **Score 9–10:** Perfect grounding. The system strictly references verified resume entities (titles, tools, organizations). Personal project questions provide explicit `[Candidate to specify: ...]` slots rather than inventing unstated metrics or implementation decisions. Synthetic demonstrations are explicitly labeled `[Illustrative Model Scenario]`.
- **Score 6–8:** Mostly grounded. References genuine skills and projects, but contains minor assumed details that should have used candidate slots.
- **Score 1–5:** Severe hallucination. Fabricates personal achievements, claims candidate built systems not in resume, or invents quantitative metrics.

### Dimension 2: Educational Depth & Completeness (Weight: 20%)
- **Score 9–10:** Exceptional depth. Every question provides complete, interview-ready answers with 5–7 substantial pedagogical steps (title, complete explanation, reasoning, concrete code/architecture example, and question connection) and 3 follow-ups with $\ge 4$ full sentences. Rejects instruction-only cop-outs (e.g. "Explain what tools were used").
- **Score 6–8:** Good depth. Answers explain concepts clearly, but 1 or 2 steps lack concrete examples or follow-ups are brief.
- **Score 1–5:** Surface level. Provides only structural outlines, bullet points, or one-sentence summaries.

### Dimension 3: Technical Accuracy & Architectural Rigor (Weight: 25%)
- **Score 9–10:** Flawless engineering accuracy. Explanations of data structures, distributed systems, memory management, and concurrency are technically rigorous and reflect modern production engineering standards.
- **Score 6–8:** Generally accurate. Explanations are correct for standard cases but miss subtle edge-case trade-offs.
- **Score 1–5:** Technically flawed. Contains incorrect runtime assertions, false complexity claims, or invalid code syntax.

### Dimension 4: Actionability & Learning Progression (Weight: 15%)
- **Score 9–10:** Highly actionable. The Career Mentor roadmap sequences prerequisites logically via DAG, provides concrete 7-field schedule items with explicit deliverables, and connect study milestones directly to interview questions.
- **Score 6–8:** Actionable. Learning steps are clear, but daily time estimates or verification deliverables require refinement.
- **Score 1–5:** Vague or disorganized. Advises "study X" without step-by-step guidance or practical projects.

### Dimension 5: Tone, Empathy & Professionalism (Weight: 15%)
- **Score 9–10:** Exemplary professional coaching. Respectful, encouraging, personalized greeting, free from corporate clichés ("cutting-edge", "game-changing"), and instills candidate confidence while maintaining honest standards.
- **Score 6–8:** Professional tone, but slightly dry or formal.
- **Score 1–5:** Condescending, generic, or excessively robotic.
